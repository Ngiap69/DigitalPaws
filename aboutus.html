About DigitalPaws

DigitalPaws is a smart pet gadget company that combines technology with the love for animals. Our goal is to make pet care easier, safer and more convenient for pet owners. We provide innovative products such as automatic pet feeders, smart water fountains, pet cameras and GPS trackers. We believe technology can help owners take better care of their pets, even when they are away from home.
